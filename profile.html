01 / Profile

Driven by ideas.
Grounded in people.

Sigge is a 22-year-old marketer from Stockholm. Social, motivated and driven, with a calm approach to challenges and a strong ability to find practical solutions.

Growing up in Stockholm and spending significant parts of his younger years abroad created an early curiosity for different cultures, behaviours and perspectives. These experiences continue to influence how he approaches communication, audiences and brand development.

The main interest lies in understanding why people make certain choices — and how brands can use that understanding to communicate more clearly, build stronger relationships and create sustainable growth.

The preferred working environment combines idea development, strategy and analysis. A place where creative thinking is supported by insight, and where data is used to make ideas stronger rather than replace them.

03 / Experience

Service created the foundation.
Marketing became the direction.

Sigge’s professional background includes working as a chef, extensive experience within restaurants and customer service, and holding marketing responsibility for a small business.

Working in restaurants developed the ability to remain calm under pressure, communicate clearly and solve problems quickly. It also built a strong understanding of service and the importance of every interaction in shaping a customer’s overall experience.

The transition into marketing built naturally on that foundation. Both service and marketing begin with the same question: what does the person on the other side actually need?

Experience from a smaller company also provided insight into the practical side of marketing. When resources are limited, priorities need to be clear, ideas realistic and communication genuinely valuable.
