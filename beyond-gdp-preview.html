Beyond GDP.

Is higher GDP per capita associated with longer life expectancy?

01 / The research

Two APIs.
One global pattern.

GDP data from the World Bank was combined with life expectancy data from the World Health Organization. Country codes connected both datasets across 179 countries.

02 / The relationship

Wealth matters.
But it is not everything.

Countries with higher GDP per capita generally have longer life expectancy. The outliers show that economic wealth does not explain the entire outcome.

GDP per capitaLife expectancy

A strong positive relationship, with important exceptions.

The logarithmic correlation is 0.83. The highlighted countries show why the pattern should open new questions rather than be treated as a complete explanation.

Paper prototype / Concept only

From insight to product idea.

The analysis became the foundation for Beyond GDP — a proposed interactive tool that helps students, journalists and analysts explore global patterns, compare countries and discover results that differ from the expected relationship.

The product concept

“What can a country’s economy tell us — and what can’t it tell us?”
The concept visualises patterns without claiming causation. It is presented as a paper prototype, not a finished digital product.
